# Presentation standard

Requested by the project owner on November 9, 2026: competition videos and demonstrations should feel emotional, natural and human.

Build the story around a specific problem, a moment of uncertainty, a concrete choice, and a visible result. Use conversational English, deliberate pauses and changes in emphasis. Actual interactions should carry the story. Avoid a monotonous feature inventory. Listen to the complete final narration and check pronunciation, timing, subtitles and audio balance before publication.

Synthetic narration must be disclosed. It must not impersonate the owner or fabricate lived experience, testimonials or measured project impact.
